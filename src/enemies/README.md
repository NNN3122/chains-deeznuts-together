# Enemies

One script per monster, all original designs, all simulated on the server with replicated state and visuals that run on every peer. Every monster that hunts a player exposes `hunt_peer` and `is_hunting()`, sits in group "monster", and answers `blast()` (a bomb went off) and `release(killed)` (let go of whoever it holds). Kills send the whole group back to the checkpoint through the level's `request_layoff`.

- `hollow_hire.gd`: Level 1. One on B4 and B3, three on B2 and B1 (`YellowRooms.HIRES_PER_FLOOR`), wandering cell to cell through the maze (`YellowRooms.route`). Sight hunters: a player in the view cone with line of sight (range shrinks crouched, grows with a lit flashlight) starts a chase at 5.6 m/s; loud noises draw them to look; Break Rooms are safe. A grab pins the victim (bracing teammates, a flashlight in its face or one shot free them; 3.5 s is a layoff). The flashlight stuns one for 3 s (then 4 s of immunity); two Snubby rounds or a bomb kill it; the floor respawns a dead one out of sight after 45 s. Also in group "hire" (the lights gutter near them). B3 also has the original Custodian.
- `custodian.gd`: B3 only. The original blind ceiling hunter: follows loud noises, appears as a stain and dust, and reaches down to lift victims. Brace nearby, burn its stain/arm with a flashlight, shoot twice, or bomb it to force a retreat. Break rooms block both hearing and grabs; regroup releases its victim and forces retreat.
- `pappy.gd`: Level 2. Pappy Pepperoni, the chef animatronic. Walks the floor the Line is on, one node per Step, only while unobserved (no player's view cone with line of sight, no live camera on his node). Lunges and grabs the nearest player within reach. A held shutter stops him for three bangs. Countered by eyes, cameras, shutters, the air horn (stun), the party popper (one node back) and a flashlight in his face while he holds someone. Guns do nothing.
- `launcher.gd`: Level 3. The Jumper, a spider the size of a sofa (the class keeps its old name, `Launcher`, and its group "launcher"). Scuttles at 1.3 m/s; inside 6 m it screeches and coils for 0.6 s, then throws itself 5-6 m along the line it had when it coiled. Step out of the line (a sprint, a slide under it) and it misses. A hit pins the victim: teammates brace beside them (`resist_value`), a flashlight in its eyes, the air horn or two Snubby hits free them; 5 s unrescued is a layoff. Three hits or a bomb kill it. Also answers `stun(seconds)`. On the rooftops it swings across gaps on a thread (`SWING`), climbs walls when its target is above (`CLIMB`), and dies if a jump drops it into the street.
- `horde/`: Level 3's swarm. `horde.gd` keeps up to 2400 mutated insects as packed arrays (no nodes, no physics), moved on `horde_field.gd`'s flow field toward the nearest chained player with separation and a pile-up climb at chokepoints, drawn as one MultiMesh per kind whose vertex shader (`horde_mesh.gd`) animates every body from four floats of custom data. Kinds: Roaches (knee-high, 3.3 m/s, they climb each other), Weavers (dog-sized spiders, 4.7 m/s, they hang on the chain), Flitters (bats, 7.5 m/s, fly over everything, circle at 4 m and dive to head height; never on the chain). The nearest 250 are "active" (claws, lunges or dives, hands on the chain, shootable); the rest are mass. Three on one chain segment stop the team; a jerk or a sprint flings them off; three sets of claws on one player for 2.5 s is a layoff. The field knows rooftops (FLOOR with a height), walls and the street between buildings (VOID): roaches stay on their roof, Weavers climb higher roofs' faces and swing gaps on threads. Anything left 110 m behind the Line goes back into the walls. The level spawns waves (`HordeArena._build_waves`, one per roof, replicated count). Group "horde", not "monster".

Rules from `CLAUDE.md`: original designs only, server-simulated, reacts to player noise, and the chain must be part of the counter.
