CHAINS DEEZNUTS TOGETHER
========================

A 1-6 player co-op horror-comedy. You are chained to your friends.
Climb out of the building. Stay quiet: something lives in the ceiling.


PLAY
----
1. Unzip first: right-click the zip, choose "Extract All...".
2. Open the new folder and double-click "CHAINS DEEZNUTS TOGETHER.exe".
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".


JOIN A FRIEND
-------------
1. Your friend clicks HOST CO-OP and sends you an invite code. It looks like K7QM-2XR.
2. Copy the code, click JOIN CO-OP, click PASTE, then CONNECT.
You can join any time, even in the middle of a run.

If the game says your copy is a different version, download the newest one again from
https://github.com/NNN3122/chains-deeznuts-together (or ask your friend for their latest
"CHAINS DEEZNUTS TOGETHER.pck", put it in this folder and replace the old one).


HOST YOUR OWN GAME
------------------
Click HOST CO-OP, press Esc, click COPY INVITE and send the code to your friends.
If Windows Firewall asks about the game, click "Allow".


CONTROLS
--------
WASD                   Move
Up / Down arrows       Walk forward / back
Left / Right, Q / E    Turn
Mouse                  Look (over TeamViewer: hold it at the side of the window to keep turning)
Shift                  Run (loud)
Ctrl or C              Brace and sneak: anchor the chain, move quietly
Space                  Jump; jump into a ledge to climb it
F or left click        Use item
G                      Drop item (hand it to a teammate)
R                      Auto-walk on/off
V                      First / third person
H                      Show / hide controls
Esc                    Pause, invite code, settings, regroup, leave

Too dark? Esc > Settings has a Brightness slider. Sound captions tell you what you heard
and from which side.


HOW TO SURVIVE
--------------
- Climb four floors, B4 to B1. Follow the green "THIS WAY" signs over doorways.
- The thing in the ceiling hunts by sound. Running, gunshots, hard landings, jetpacks and a
  yanked chain are loud. Walk, or sneak with Ctrl.
- If it grabs someone, everyone near them holds Ctrl to brace. Or shine a flashlight up at it,
  or shoot it twice.
- The squeaky decoy sends it somewhere else. The bomb launches everyone nearby, including
  the people you're chained to.
- A dangling teammate drags you toward the edge unless you brace or keep walking to hoist them.
- Lost or stuck? Esc, then "Stuck? Regroup" pulls the whole team back to the last break room.
