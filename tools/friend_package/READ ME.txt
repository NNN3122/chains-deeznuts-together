CHAINS DEEZNUTS TOGETHER
========================

A 1-10 player co-op horror-comedy. You are chained to your friends.
Climb out of the building. Stay quiet: something lives in the ceiling.
This build has two levels: the Yellow Rooms, then Pappy Pepperoni's Night Shift
(a haunted pizzeria, stacked on top).


PLAY
----
1. Unzip first: right-click the zip, choose "Extract All...".
2. Open the new folder and double-click "CHAINS DEEZNUTS TOGETHER.exe".
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".


JOIN A FRIEND
-------------
1. Your friend clicks HOST CO-OP and sends you an invite code. It looks like K7QM-2XR.
2. Copy the code, click JOIN CO-OP, click PASTE, then CONNECT.
You can join any time, even in the middle of a run.

If the game says your copy is a different version, download the newest one again from
https://github.com/NNN3122/chains-deeznuts-together (or ask your friend for their latest
"CHAINS DEEZNUTS TOGETHER.pck", put it in this folder and replace the old one).


HOST YOUR OWN GAME
------------------
Click HOST CO-OP, press Esc, click COPY INVITE and send the code to your friends.
Up to 10 players (you and 9 friends). If Windows Firewall asks about the game, click "Allow".


CONTROLS
--------
WASD                   Move
Up / Down arrows       Walk forward / back
Left / Right, Q / E    Turn
Mouse                  Look (over TeamViewer: hold it at the side of the window to keep turning)
Shift                  Run (loud)
Ctrl or C              Crouch: brace the chain, sneak quietly, fit under low things
Space                  Jump; jump into a ledge to climb it
F or left click        Use the item in your hands
1 / 2 / 3, wheel       Pick which of your three items you hold
G                      Drop the item in your hands (hand it to a teammate)
T                      Reload the Snubby (6 rounds in, a pocket of spares; ammo boxes refill it)
R                      Auto-walk on/off
V                      First / third person
H                      Show / hide controls
Esc                    Pause, invite code, settings, regroup, leave

Too dark? Esc > Settings has a Brightness slider. Sound captions tell you what you heard
and from which side. Back rooms are meant to be dark: that's what the flashlight is for.
Voice chat: just talk. Friends within about 20 m hear you, muffled through walls.
The checklist under the objective (top left) ticks off what to do next.
Don't camp: stand still too long out in the open and the thing in the ceiling comes to look.
If the chef is banging on a shutter you're holding, HOLD IT for all three bangs. Let go early
and he comes through at a run. Same when the power is out: run for a break room.


HOW TO SURVIVE
--------------
- Climb four floors, B4 to B1. Follow the green "THIS WAY" signs over doorways.
- The thing in the ceiling hunts by sound. Running, gunshots, hard landings, jetpacks and a
  yanked chain are loud. Walk, or sneak with Ctrl.
- If it grabs someone, everyone near them holds Ctrl to brace. Or shine a flashlight up at it,
  or shoot it twice.
- The squeaky decoy sends it somewhere else. The bomb launches everyone nearby, including
  the people you're chained to.
- A dangling teammate drags you toward the edge unless you brace or keep walking to hoist them.
- Lost or stuck? Esc, then "Stuck? Regroup" pulls the whole team back to the last break room.
- Everyone into the freight elevator on B1 and it rides you up into the pizzeria.

THE NIGHT SHIFT (UPSTAIRS)
--------------------------
- Pappy Pepperoni only moves while nobody is looking at him and no camera shows him.
  One of you keeps eyes on him, the rest move. The top-right panel says if he's pinned.
- Cameras: stand on the painted spot at a security desk, F flips cameras. A camera on his
  room pins him too. Cameras drain the power; at 0% the lights die and nothing stops him.
  Stand in a break room a few seconds to restart the generator.
- Shutters: hold Ctrl at the pull chain to keep the EMPLOYEES ONLY shutter down. He bangs
  three times and leaves. Then run for it.
- The dumbwaiter takes one rider; someone holds Ctrl at the hand rope to haul (alone: step
  in and hold Ctrl). The stage lift runs while someone holds Ctrl at the lever; three ride,
  the last one gets hoisted up on the chain.
- If he grabs someone: air horn, party popper, or a flashlight in his face. Guns do nothing.
  The walk-in freezer is safe.
- The way out: cross the stage, climb the speaker case to the prop loft. Everyone up there.
